1. Mission Alignment

Crossing Sunsets™ is dedicated to creating transparency for families navigating the complexities of post-acute care transitions. Our platform provides the verifiable data needed to ensure dignity during The Last Journey Transition.

2. Terms of Use & Legal Disclaimers

Crossing Sunsets™ acts solely as a connecting platform (directory) between families/individuals and third-party care providers (such as palliative care teams and skilled nursing facilities) specializing in The Last Journey Transition.

  • Not a Healthcare Provider: We do not provide medical or clinical services.
  • No Employment Relationship: We do not employ the providers listed on this site.
  • No Operational Control: We do not direct or control the day-to-day operations, medical protocols, or care decisions of any third-party provider.
  • Platform Purpose: This site is a B2B standards authority and directory. Users are responsible for their own due diligence.

3. The Lotus Review System™ (CPR = S)

Our proprietary rating system is based on three Standards of Care excellence:

  • • Compassionate: Kind, Attentive, Emotionally supportive presence.
  • • Professional: Skilled, accountable, reliable care delivery.
  • • Responsive: Timely communication and follow-through.

Result: CPR = S (SAFE)

4. Executive Support

This platform is maintained for authorized enterprise partners and active M&A licensing reviews. We maintain strict domain authority to protect the integrity of the care standard.
